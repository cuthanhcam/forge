# Supporting Forge

Forge is an MIT-licensed open-source project. Financial support is optional:

- [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/cuthanhcam)
- [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/cuthanhcam)

These are the maintainer destinations configured in `.github/FUNDING.yml`.
Payments are handled by the external provider; Forge does not collect payment
details. Sponsorship does not purchase a support SLA, security certification,
feature priority or influence over vulnerability disclosure.

Non-financial contributions are equally welcome: improve docs, test a small
screen or keyboard workflow, report a reproducible bug with synthetic input,
or submit a focused fix through [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).
